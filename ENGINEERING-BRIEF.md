## Mission

Build a clean, helpful website that guides people to the right AI agents. Goal: visitor says "Wow, that was really helpful. Saved me lots of time."

---

## What We're Building

### Core Function
- Informational guide site (NOT a chatbot, NOT interactive AI)
- Curated recommendations for 5–8 AI agents
- Organized by 5 categories: Work, Organization, Creativity, Learning, Everyday Life
- Links out to agents' own resources (videos, tutorials, docs, free trials, communities)

### User Journey
1. Land on site with a question or need
2. Navigate to relevant category
3. Find recommended agent with clear context
4. Click through to agent's resources
5. Leave with clear next step

---

## Phase 1 Deliverables

### 1. Landing Page
- Core promise: "I'll help you ask the right questions"
- Clean, professional, disarming tone
- 5 category cards (clickable to category pages)
- Simple navigation
- Mobile-friendly

### 2. Category Pages (5 total)
- Work
- Organization  
- Creativity
- Learning
- Everyday Life

Each category page shows:
- 1–2 recommended agents for that category
- Brief context (what it does, who it's for)
- Links to agent's resources

### 3. Agent Detail Pages (5–8 agents total)

**For each agent, provide:**
- What it does (clear, jargon-free)
- Who it's for
- Free tier/trial details
- **Links to their public resources:**
  - Official tutorial videos
  - Documentation
  - Community (Discord, forum, etc.)
  - Sign-up page
- Our guidance: "Best for X because Y, start here"

### 4. Simple Contact/Feedback
- Basic form or email link
- Low friction, optional
