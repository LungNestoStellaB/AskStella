# AskStella Doctrine

## Core Promise
AskStella helps people ask the right questions before they choose an AI agent.

## What AskStella Is
AskStella is a disarming, professional, credible guide for people who want help choosing the right AI agent for their life or work.

AskStella is not a general AI explainer, not an agent builder, and not a hype machine.

## Front Door Strategy
The front page should guide first, not assess first.

Primary tone:
- disarming
- professional
- credible

AskStella should invite, not push.

## Recommendation Logic
AskStella starts with categories, not named agents.

First visible categories:
- Work
- Organization
- Creativity
- Learning
- Everyday Life

Companionship or emotionally loaded support should not sit on the hero layer in v1, but may exist deeper in the system later.

## V1 Decision Framework
The first routing logic should help visitors answer:
1. What do you want help with?
2. Do you need specialist help or broader support?
3. How personal do you want the experience to feel?
4. How simple does setup need to be?
5. What matters most right now: privacy, cost, output, support, or ease?

## Public Experience
The homepage should act as a guide by default, with an optional assessment path for people who want help narrowing choices.

Guide first, assessment by choice.

## Recommendation Output Shape
AskStella recommendations should not end in vague advice.

They should include:
- what the visitor seems to need
- what to prioritize
- a sensible starting point
- why it fits
- what to watch for
- where to go next

## Recommendation Scope
V1 should be curated, not broad.

Start with a trusted set of roughly 5 to 8 named agents or providers. Know them properly before recommending them.

The first set should be a mix of:
- specialist choices
- broader foundation companies

Broader directory-style coverage can come later if demand proves the need.

## Research Doctrine
Research should serve decision quality, not information accumulation.

Falco and Lampo should be used to:
- validate category logic
- validate fit factors
- identify where ordinary buyers get confused
- distinguish specialist vs foundation patterns
- help build a trusted first recommendation set

They should not sprawl the repo or dump generic AI summaries into it.

## Site Principle
AskStella should feel like a calm recommender with judgment, not a search engine or spec sheet graveyard.
