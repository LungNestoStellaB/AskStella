## SUBJECT 1 — What Is an AI Agent, Actually?
**Category:** Foundational

---

### PART A — 45-Second Short

You've heard the word "agent" everywhere lately.
But nobody's actually explaining what it means.

An AI agent isn't a robot.
It's not a chatbot that just answers questions.

It's a tool that works on your behalf.
You give it a job. It figures out how to do it.
You get the result.

That's it.

No coding. No technical knowledge required.
Just a clear ask — and something that actually follows through.

The question isn't whether AI agents are real.
They are.

The question is: which one is right for you?

That's exactly what AskStella is here to help you figure out.

---

### PART B — 3m15 Mid-Form

**Beat 1 — The Confusion**

Let's clear something up.

Because the word "agent" has been thrown around so much
that most people have no idea what it actually means.

And that confusion is costing people.
Not money — time.
Time spent avoiding something that could genuinely change how they work.

So let's start from zero.

An AI agent is a piece of software that acts on your behalf.
You give it a goal.
It breaks that goal into steps.
It takes those steps.
It comes back with a result.

That's the whole thing.

It's not magic. It's not sentient.
It doesn't know you, it doesn't judge you,
and it definitely doesn't care if you ask it the same question three times.

---

**Beat 2 — The Difference That Matters**

Here's where people get confused.

They think an AI agent is just a smarter search engine.
Or a fancier autocomplete.

It's not.

A search engine finds information.
An AI agent does something with it.

You ask a search engine: "how do I write a cover letter?"
You ask an AI agent: "Write me a cover letter for this job. Here's my background."

One gives you a list of articles.
The other hands you a draft.

That's the shift.

From looking things up — to getting things done.

And once you feel that shift for the first time,
you don't go back.

The people who've already made that shift?
They're not tech experts.
They're people who got tired of doing everything the hard way.

---

**Beat 3 — The Only Question Left**

So now you know what an AI agent is.

The next question — the only one that actually matters — is:
which one is right for you?

Because there are hundreds of them.
And most of them are built for very specific things.

Some are built for work.
Some for creativity.
Some for learning.
Some just for making daily life a little less exhausting.

You don't need all of them.
You need one.
Maybe two.

The right one, used well, is a genuine equalizer.

That's what AskStella is built for.
Not to overwhelm you with options —
to help you find the one that fits your life.

Just ask AskStella.

## SUBJECT 2 — Why Would I Need One? (And Why You Might Not)
**Category:** Foundational

---

### PART A — 45-Second Short

Here's something you won't hear very often:

You might not need an AI agent right now.

And that's completely fine.

AI agents are built for people with a specific kind of problem —
something repetitive, time-consuming, or just outside their skill set.

If your life is running smoothly, there's no rush.

But if there's something you keep putting off
because it feels too hard, too slow, or too complicated —
that's exactly where an agent earns its place.

Not because it's impressive.
Because it gets the thing done.

Wondering if that's you?
AskStella can help you figure it out.

---

### PART B — 3m15 Mid-Form

**Beat 1 — The Honest Question**

Let's ask the question nobody asks first.

Do you actually need an AI agent?

Because most of the conversation around AI skips straight to
"here's how to use it" — without ever stopping to ask
whether you should bother in the first place.

And that's a problem.

Because if you start with the wrong tool for the wrong reason,
you'll spend a week trying to make it work,
decide it's not for you,
and walk away from something that could have genuinely helped.

So let's do this properly.

AI agents are built for a specific kind of situation.
They shine when something is repetitive, time-consuming,
or requires a skill you don't have and don't want to learn.

Writing. Research. Organising. Summarising. Planning.
These are the lanes where agents are genuinely strong.

---

**Beat 2 — The Signs It's For You**

Here's how you know an agent might be worth your time.

You have a task you keep avoiding.
Not because you don't care — because it drains you every time.

You spend hours on something that feels like it should take minutes.

You're good at your work but the admin around it is killing you.

You want to learn something but don't know where to start.

Any of those land?

That's the signal.

An agent won't replace your judgment.
It won't make decisions for you.
But it will take the heavy lifting off your plate
so you can focus on the part only you can do.

That's not a small thing.
For a lot of people, that's the difference between
getting something done and never getting to it at all.

---

**Beat 3 — And If It's Not For You Yet**

Now — what if none of that landed?

What if your life is running fine and you're just curious?

That's a completely valid place to be.

You don't have to adopt anything.
You don't have to sign up for anything.
You can just learn what's out there,
understand what these tools actually do,
and come back when the moment is right.

AskStella isn't here to push you through a door you're not ready for.

It's here so that when you are ready —
you know exactly which door to walk through.

Just ask AskStella.

## SUBJECT 3 — The 5 Things People Get Wrong About AI Agents
**Category:** Foundational

---

### PART A — 45-Second Short

Before you try an AI agent, let's clear up five things
that almost everyone gets wrong.

It's not going to replace you.
It's not reading your mind.
It's not always right.
It's not only for tech people.
And it's definitely not going to cost you a fortune to start.

Those five beliefs are the reason most people either
never try — or try once and give up too fast.

Once you know the truth behind each one,
the whole thing gets a lot less intimidating.

AskStella breaks all five down.
So you can start with the right expectations
and actually get somewhere.

---

### PART B — 3m15 Mid-Form

**Beat 1 — Why Expectations Matter**

The number one reason people fail with AI agents
has nothing to do with the technology.

It's expectations.

They go in thinking it'll be magic —
and when it's not, they decide it doesn't work.

Or they go in thinking it's too complicated —
and they never start at all.

Both of those are wrong.
And both of them are fixable.

So here are the five things most people get wrong —
and what's actually true.

---

**Beat 2 — The Five Myths**

One. It's going to replace me.

No. An agent handles tasks. You handle judgment.
It does the heavy lifting. You decide what matters.
That's a partnership, not a replacement.

Two. It knows what I want without me explaining.

It doesn't. The clearer you are, the better it performs.
Vague in, vague out. Specific in, specific out.
That's not a flaw — that's just how it works.

Three. It's always right.

It's not. AI agents make mistakes.
They can misunderstand, miss context, or get things wrong.
You're still the one who checks the work.
Think of it as a very capable assistant — not an oracle.

Four. It's only for tech people.

This one is the most damaging myth of all.
The best agents are built for people who are not technical.
If you can type a sentence, you can use one.

Five. It's going to cost me money to try.

Most agents have a free tier.
You can test, explore, and decide — before spending a cent.

---

**Beat 3 — Now You're Ready**

Here's what changes when you know all five.

You stop waiting for magic and start working with what's real.
You stop being vague and start being specific.
You check the output instead of trusting it blindly.
You stop thinking it's not for you.
And you start for free.

That's the difference between someone who tries once and quits
and someone who builds a genuine working relationship with a tool
that makes their life measurably better.

You're already ahead of most people just by knowing this.

AskStella can help you take the next step.
Just ask.

## SUBJECT 4 — How to Know If You're Ready to Use One
**Category:** Foundational

---

### PART A — 45-Second Short

Wondering if you're ready to try an AI agent?

Here's a quick check.

You don't need to be technical.
You don't need to have tried AI before.
You don't need a lot of time.

What you do need:
one task you want to do better or faster,
a willingness to try something once,
and the patience to give it a fair go.

That's genuinely it.

If you have those three things,
you're ready.

And if you're not sure which agent to start with —
that's what AskStella is here for.

---

### PART B — 3m15 Mid-Form

**Beat 1 — The Readiness Question**

"Am I ready for this?"

It's the question most people ask quietly —
and never actually answer.

Because there's no quiz. No checklist. No certificate.
Nobody tells you when you're ready for a new tool.

So people wait.
They watch. They read. They think about it.
And the months go by.

Here's the truth:
readiness for an AI agent isn't about skill level.
It's about having a clear enough problem to solve.

That's the only real requirement.

---

**Beat 2 — The Three Things You Actually Need**

Let's make this concrete.

First — one task.
Not a life overhaul. Not a new system.
Just one thing you want to do better, faster, or with less effort.
Writing something. Researching something. Organising something.
One task is enough to start.

Second — willingness to try once.
Not commitment. Not a subscription.
Just the openness to sit down with a tool for twenty minutes
and see what happens.

Third — patience with the learning curve.
The first time you use any new tool, it feels awkward.
That's not failure. That's just first-time friction.
Give it three sessions before you decide anything.

If you have those three things —
a task, a willingness, and a little patience —
you're ready.

---

**Beat 3 — What Ready Actually Looks Like**

Ready doesn't mean confident.
Ready doesn't mean you know what you're doing.

Ready just means you're willing to find out.

The people who get the most out of AI agents
aren't the ones who waited until they felt prepared.
They're the ones who started before they felt ready
and figured it out as they went.

You don't need to know everything before you begin.
You just need to begin.

And if you want a hand picking the right starting point —
AskStella is right here.
Just ask.

AI agents aren't one-size-fits-all.
They're built for specific parts of your life.

Most people belong to one or two of these worlds.
And the agents built for your world
are completely different from the ones built for someone else's.

So before you pick a tool —
figure out which category your problem lives in.

That one step saves you hours of trying the wrong thing.

AskStella maps it all out.
Tell us where you live — we'll show you the door.

---

### PART B — 3m15 Mid-Form

**Beat 1 — Why Categories Matter**

Here's a mistake almost everyone makes.

They hear about an AI agent — usually the most famous one —
and they try it.

It doesn't quite fit.
It's not bad. It's just not built for what they need.

So they assume AI agents aren't for them.

But the problem wasn't the technology.
The problem was the match.

AI agents are highly specialised.
The one built for a novelist is different from the one built for an accountant.
The one built for a student is different from the one built for a retiree.

Knowing your category is the first real step.

AskStella organises everything into five categories.

Work.

Organisation.

Creativity.

Learning.
